# Supplier Evaluation

Use this reference when deciding whether a 1688 shop is worth asking.

## Strong Source-Factory Signals

- Super factory or clear factory certification on 1688.
- Long operating years on 1688.
- Main category matches the target product category.
- The shop has many related products in the same category instead of unrelated mixed goods.
- Product page shows production, customization, sample, processing, or factory-direct language.
- MOQ, lead time, and customization options look like manufacturing terms.
- Customer service confirms factory direct, self-owned factory, or direct production.

## Reseller or Weak Supplier Signals

- Very mixed product categories with no clear manufacturing focus.
- Many unrelated listings copied across categories.
- Price is much higher than comparable suppliers without a clear reason.
- The shop cannot answer MOQ, production lead time, or factory-direct questions.
- The shop redirects immediately to another contact without product details.
- The product looks similar but the shop's main category does not match.

## Evidence Rules

- Do not decide from one signal alone.
- "超级工厂" is a strong positive signal, not absolute proof.
- Low price is useful only after product match and supplier credibility are acceptable.
- If evidence is incomplete, mark the supplier as "待确认" instead of rejecting too early.
- After replies arrive, re-rank using actual MOQ, price, lead time, and direct-factory answer.

## Practical Ranking Language

Use these judgement labels in the chat summary and export:

- `优先跟进`: strong category match, credible factory signals, and useful inquiry response.
- `可备选`: product match is acceptable, but factory evidence or reply is incomplete.
- `暂不建议`: category mismatch, reseller-like behavior, weak evidence, or poor inquiry response.
- `待回复`: inquiry sent but no useful response yet.
